I am <i class="proper-name">Adrian Junge</i> (<i class="proper-name">vurlo</i>). I started programming during school and began exploring cybersecurity with [<i class="proper-name">OverTheWire</i>](https://overthewire.org/wargames/). I later solved different rooms on [<i class="proper-name">TryHackMe</i>](https://tryhackme.com/p/vurlo) and also several challenges in the legendary [<i class="proper-name">OWASP Juice Shop</i>](https://github.com/juice-shop/juice-shop). While studying at university I became more interested in solving algorithmic problems on [<i class="proper-name">LeetCode</i>](https://leetcode.com/u/vurlo), but still never lost my passion for CTF challenges and started solving my first [<i class="proper-name">CSCG</i>](https://cscg.de/) challenges. Have a look [how I got started](/timeline?order=oldest#timeline-event-overthewire-beginnings) and kept advancing my skills.

Today I study computer science at [<i class="proper-name">KIT Karlsruhe Institute of Technology</i>](https://www.kit.edu/) with a focus on cybersecurity and play CTFs as part of [<i class="proper-name">KITCTF</i>](https://kitctf.de/). Next to my studies, I worked as a research assistant and working student at [<i class="proper-name">Fraunhofer IOSB</i>](https://www.iosb.fraunhofer.de/en.html), [<i class="proper-name">1&amp;1 Mail &amp; Media</i>](https://www.mail-and-media.com/en/), and [<i class="proper-name">FZI Forschungszentrum für Informatik</i>](https://www.fzi.de/).
