I am <i class="proper-name">Adrian Junge</i> (<i class="proper-name">vurlo</i>). I started programming in school, and back then I could not have imagined how deep that rabbit hole would go. I first became interested in algorithms through competitive programming and solving problems on [<i class="proper-name">LeetCode</i>](https://leetcode.com/). Over time that problem-solving curiosity pulled me further into cybersecurity. Today I study computer science at [<i class="proper-name">KIT Karlsruhe Institute of Technology</i>](https://www.kit.edu/) with a focus on cybersecurity and play CTFs as part of [<i class="proper-name">KITCTF</i>](https://kitctf.de/), the university's CTF team. Alongside my studies, I worked as a research assistant and working student at [<i class="proper-name">Fraunhofer IOSB</i>](https://www.iosb.fraunhofer.de/en.html), [<i class="proper-name">1&amp;1 Mail &amp; Media</i>](https://www.mail-and-media.com/en/), and [<i class="proper-name">FZI Forschungszentrum für Informatik</i>](https://www.fzi.de/). Lately, I have been digging deeper into bug bounty hunting, coordinated disclosure, and CVE disclosures.

This page is meant as a record of what I have worked on and learned from: reproduced bugs, writeups, disclosures, certificates, and a few milestones along the way.
